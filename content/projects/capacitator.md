## Capacitator

Helps Agile teams calculate their sprint capacity by considering working days, team size, and reserved capacity.

![Capacitator Screenshot](/images/capacitator.png)

### Features
- Sprint capacity calculation
- Team size configuration
- Holiday and time-off consideration
- Reserved capacity management
- Easy-to-use interface

### Technology
- Web-based calculator
- JavaScript
