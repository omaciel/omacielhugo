## Potentializer

HTML-based talent management tool that allows managers to categorize team members across a 9-box grid.

![Potentializer Screenshot](/images/potentializer.png)

### Features
- Interactive 9-box grid interface
- Drag-and-drop team member management
- Visual talent assessment and succession planning
- Export capabilities for reports

### Technology
- HTML/CSS/JavaScript
- Client-side application
