## Trying Something New

Yesterday, I resisted the urge to work on a desktop app and instead went shopping with my wife. While browsing, we discovered the "I Should Have Known That!" trivia game.

![Trivia Game](/images/i-should-have-known-that-game.heic)

## Childhood Memories

This discovery brought back memories of childhood when I would play board games with my sisters. We'd spend hours playing [Monopoly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monopoly_(game)) and [Canasta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canasta), creating memories that I've cherished for decades.

I've long held a desire to create a similar tradition with my own family—a regular game night where we could all disconnect from screens and connect with each other.

## Previous Attempts

Despite previous attempts with games like [Uno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uno_(card_game)) and [Cat Crimes](https://www.ravensburger.us/en-US/products/games/thinkfun/cat-crimes-44001550), we had struggled to maintain a consistent game night. As our daughters grew older, finding an activity everyone enjoyed became increasingly challenging.

## A New Hope

The trivia game sparked a new opportunity. During a family lunch, we decided to give it a try. There were hilarious moments, like when my wife gave a hint about Arnold Schwarzenegger's birthplace by saying "Austria-la vista, baby!"

We played three games together and enjoyed it so much that we decided to make it a regular activity.

## Building Traditions

I'm hopeful that this might become a cherished family tradition, reminiscent of those wonderful game nights from my childhood. Sometimes the best traditions start with a simple "yes" to trying something new together.

Who knows? Maybe years from now, my daughters will have their own families and remember these trivia game nights with the same fondness I have for those Monopoly marathons with my sisters.
