Hi, I’m Og.

I learned BASIC on a TK90X, typing programs out of Input magazine in the far north of Brazil. Today I lead software engineers at Red Hat, and write novels in two languages. The Big Score took first place for Young Adult in Portuguese at the 2023 International Latino Book Awards.
