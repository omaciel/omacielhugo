Three directions for omaciel.github.io

Each mockup uses real content from the site: recent posts, the five book covers, the Latino Book Awards win, and the Life in Rough Draft newsletter. Open the home page and a post page for each, and try them on a phone-width window too.

1. Rough Draft

Named after your newsletter. The site is a yellow legal pad: text sits on the ruled lines, dates live in the red margin, and the headline carries a live edit, with “aspiring” struck through and “award-winning” written in above it.

HomePost

2. Azulejo

Brazilian modernism, after Athos Bulcão’s tile walls in Brasília, with your animated avatar up front. Yellow and green tiles mark your series: The Mill, Murder Hornet, and Ikigai.

HomePost

3. TK90X

Based on the Brazilian ZX Spectrum clone you learned BASIC on. The screen plays a loading border, then types out a BASIC program about you. Everything else is set in Atkinson Hyperlegible, with filters for fiction, work, life, and reading.
