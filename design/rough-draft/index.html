Og Maciel

Brazilian-American software engineer and aspiringaward-winning novelist.

I was born in the northernmost corner of Brazil, landed at JFK in 1991 with one suitcase and a box of stamps, and now live in Chapel Hill, North Carolina, with my family. By day I lead software engineers at Red Hat. The rest of the time I write novels in English and Portuguese, and a newsletter called Life in Rough Draft.
